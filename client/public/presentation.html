SLIDE 02 / 05
System Capabilities
Comprehensive Platform Scope
🚢

Smart Shipment Ingestion

  • Instant auto-resolution of Bill of Lading (B/L) numbers
  • Automatic pre-fill of vessel, voyage, discharge dates & ports
  • Precise container selection with tare weights and seal verifications
🔍

Survey Operations & Triage

  • Third-party marine surveyor appointment and cost booking
  • Inspection findings, damage categorization & joint reports
  • Seamless 1-click survey conversion to formal cargo claim
💵

Multi-Currency Recoveries

  • Granular itemization of claim expenses and recoveries
  • Real-time automated foreign exchange normalization via live rates
  • Standardized executive view of net organizational liability in USD
📊

Governance & Executive KPIs

  • Automated statutory 1-year maritime time-bar expiration tracking
  • Multi-tier Row-Level Security (RLS) keeping port agency data segregated
  • Live analytics dashboards showcasing loss categories and financial exposure
SLIDE 03 / 05
Operational Journey
End-to-End Application Workflow
Step 1

Intake & Lookup

User logs in. Enters B/L number; system verifies and auto-populates all voyage & cargo specifics.

➔
Step 2

Triage & Survey

Dispatches surveyor if necessary. Once damage is confirmed, converts survey directly into formal claim.

➔
Step 3

Claim Lodging

Selects damaged containers, uploads mandatory commercial documents, triggers 1-year time-bar SLA.

➔
Step 4

Financial Tally

Logs multi-currency expenses & recoveries. Auto-converts to USD net exposure using daily exchange rates.

➔
Step 5

Resolution

MID reviews P&I deductibles and legal merits. Settles or repudiates with full audit history preserved.
